Opening-Hand Card Atlas

How much each card moves your game win rate when it is in your opening seven, and which pairs of cards do better together than apart. The opening hand is dealt at random given the deck, so these numbers aren't inflated by who drafts the card, the way 17Lands' GIH WR is.

Opening-hand value against GIH WR

GIH WR mixes card power with who plays the card: strong drafters pick some cards more, and a card looks good because its owners are good. Labelled cards sit furthest from the trend line. Above the line, GIH WR undersells the card; below it, GIH WR oversells it.

Method

Card values: results/<SET>/hand_value_v2/card_values.csv. A residual-on-propensity estimate of the effect of a card being in the opening seven, given the deck, mulligans and land count; held-out games replicate it at r ≈ 0.78. Pairs: deck_synergy_v8/hand_pairs.csv, a rank-4 pair model on opening hands fitted on top of a per-card slope, so card draw can't drive it. Pair log-odds are shown as win-rate points at a 50% baseline (Q × 25). Built by scripts/build_card_atlas.py.
