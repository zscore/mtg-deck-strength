Where the numbers come from

Model estimate Opening-hand value

A per-card effect estimated from games: win rate when the card was in the opening seven, compared with games where the same deck had it in the library, after adjusting for the rest of the deck, mulligans and land count. The hand is dealt at random given the deck, so who drafted the card doesn't leak in. Fitted by scripts/hand_value_v2.py.

Model weights Pair synergy and the four axes

Learned weights of a game-level pair model on opening hands. Each card gets a vector of four numbers, and a pair's synergy Q is the dot product of the two vectors. Fitted by scripts/deck_synergy_v8.py; the vectors are recovered exactly from its pair table by scripts/synergy_axes.py.

17Lands stat GIH WR

17Lands' "games in hand win rate", computed from the same game files. Shown only for comparison. It is raw: it mixes card strength with the skill of the players who choose the card.

Synergy space

The four axes of synergy Model weights

The pair model places every card at a point in four dimensions. Two cards pointing the same way synergise, opposite ways clash, and at right angles they don't interact. Cards far from the centre are the most synergy-dependent. Axis 1 carries the most synergy, axis 4 the least . Click a card to pin it and see its partners.

Method

Card values: results/<SET>/hand_value_v2/card_values.csv, a residual-on-propensity estimate of the effect of a card being in the opening seven, given the deck, mulligans and land count; held-out games replicate it at r ≈ 0.78. Pairs: deck_synergy_v8/hand_pairs.csv, a rank-4 pair model on opening hands fitted on top of a per-card slope, so card draw can't drive it. Axes: deck_synergy_v8/hand_axes.csv. Pair log-odds are shown as win-rate points at a 50% baseline (Q × 25). Built by scripts/build_card_atlas.py.
